Welcome to Belvisi Jensen

You start on the landing outside the front door: open it to step inside. Built from the floor plan in Blender, with oak floors and the kitchen as delivered. Camera pins open the Blender render taken from that spot. The bar on the right switches to the view from above, shows sizes, sockets, the tape measure and the map, and sets the time of day. Tap the map to jump somewhere.
